What customers should know

The short version: Texas has debated major changes to hemp-derived THC rules. Customers should expect updates, confirm current product availability in-store, and avoid relying on old screenshots or rumors.

What changed?

Texas lawmakers considered broad restrictions on hemp-derived THC products. In 2025, Senate Bill 3 would have banned consumable hemp products containing THC, including delta-8 and delta-9 categories.

What is currently on hold?

Governor Greg Abbott vetoed SB 3 in June 2025 and called for lawmakers to address hemp through regulation instead of a total ban. Future changes may still affect availability and requirements.

Why product availability may change

Retailers may adjust shelves as rules, enforcement priorities, suppliers, registration requirements, or testing standards change. Ask in-store for what is currently available and compliant.

How Lone Star Hempworks stays current

We keep customer communication grounded: adult-only retail, no medical claims, clear product labels, batch questions, and plain-language updates when rules change.
